About

As the Head of Transportation Algorithms, I have been deeply engaged in the field of intelligent transportation, focusing on foundational algorithm research and large-scale industrial implementation. My research areas include vehicle-road-cloud-map integration, single-agent perception, multi-agent collaborative perception, integrated event detection systems, emergency response, and road network monitoring. Currently, my primary focus lies in cutting-edge technologies such as multimodal video understanding, intelligent data annotation, high-value data generation powered by AIGC, large model miniaturization, and spatial intelligence. I am committed to advancing deep innovation and practical applications in intelligent transportation technologies.
